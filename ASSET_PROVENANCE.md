## Name and branding

“Spelling Speurtocht” is a descriptive Dutch phrase. A limited public web search
found the phrase used for classroom exercises, including a worksheet activity on
[Meester Maarten's spelling page](https://meestermaarten.org/lesmateriaal/spelling/).
This project does not use that site's worksheets, pictures, layout or logo, and
is presented as an independent offline application by drblahdblah.

A descriptive title and a third party's creative work are different review
subjects. The project does not claim ownership of the phrase itself, registered
trademark status, or affiliation with other teaching resources. This review is
not a formal Benelux/EU/international trademark or trade-name clearance search.
